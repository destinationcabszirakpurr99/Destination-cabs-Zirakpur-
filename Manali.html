Curated Travel Plan

4-Day Royal Manali & Solang Itinerary

Our bestseller Manali snow & adventure plan starting from Zirakpur / Chandigarh.

Day01

Zirakpur / Chandigarh to Manali • Scenic 5-Tunnel Drive

Morning pickup from your doorstep, Chandigarh Airport, or Railway Station. Drive along the scenic Bilaspur lake, Mandi, and Pandoh Dam through the new 4-lane highway tunnels. Evening check-in at Manali and leisure walk at Manali Mall Road.

Day02

Solang Snow Valley, Atal Tunnel & Sissu Waterfall

Full-day snow excursion! Morning adventure at Solang Valley (ropeway, paragliding, snow activities). Continue through the 9.02 km Atal Tunnel (10,040 ft) into Lahaul Valley to visit Sissu Lake & Waterfall.

Day03

Hadimba Temple, Old Manali Cafes & Vashisht Springs

Explore Manali’s heritage gems: Hadimba Devi Cedar Temple, Club House, vibrant Old Manali riverside cafes, Tibetan Monastery, and the natural hot water springs of Vashisht Temple.

Day04

Kullu River Rafting, Vaishno Devi Temple & Return Drop

After checkout, stop at Kullu Valley for thrilling Beas River white-water rafting, Kullu shawl weaving factories, and Hanogi Mata Viewpoint before a comfortable evening drop at Zirakpur or Chandigarh.
