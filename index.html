Curated Journeys

Explore The Extraordinary

From the snow-capped peaks of Leh to the divine temples of Char Dham and the royal forts of Jaipur, discover India's crown jewels with us.

Leh Ladakh

Conquer the world's highest passes and camp by the crystal blue Pangong Lake. The ultimate rugged luxury.

Char Dham Yatra

A divine pilgrimage to Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, and Badrinath with paramount safety and peace.

Magical Manali

Experience the snow-capped peaks of Rohtang and the thrilling adventure of Solang Valley.

Queen of Hills

Walk down the historic Ridge and enjoy the colonial heritage of Shimla and Kufri.

Spiti Valley

Explore the mystical middle land. Ancient monasteries, Key Gompa, and Chandratal lake await.

Royal Jaipur

Experience the grandeur of Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, and the rich Rajputana culture in the Pink City.

Braj Bhoomi

A divine trip to Lord Krishna's birthplace. Visit Banke Bihari, Prem Mandir, and Gokul.

Paradise Kashmir

Glide on Dal Lake in a Shikara, and witness the unparalleled beauty of Gulmarg and Pahalgam.

Agra City

Witness the symbol of eternal love, the magnificent Taj Mahal, in absolute luxury.

Golden Amritsar

Seek blessings at the Golden Temple and feel the patriotic fervor at the Wagah Border.

Mussoorie & Dehradun

Relax in the Queen of the Hills. Explore Kempty Falls, Mall Road, and scenic viewpoints.

Dalhousie & Dharamshala

Discover the Mini Switzerland of India (Khajjiar) and the spiritual home of the Dalai Lama.
