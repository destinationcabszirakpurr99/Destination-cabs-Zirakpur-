Curated Travel Plan

3-Day Royal Shimla & Kufri Itinerary

Our most popular weekend tour plan starting from Chandigarh / Zirakpur Airport or Railway Station.

Day01

Zirakpur / Chandigarh to Shimla • Colonial Heritage Walk

Morning pickup from your doorstep, Chandigarh Airport, or Railway Station. Enjoy a scenic stopover at Pinjore Mughal Gardens & Timber Trail. Check into your Shimla hotel by afternoon. Evening drop at the Tourism Lift to explore Mall Road, The Ridge, Christ Church, and Scandal Point.

Day02

Kufri Snow Point, Green Valley & Jakhoo Hill

After breakfast, embark on a full-day excursion stopping at Green Valley photo point. Spend the afternoon at Kufri Snow & Adventure Point and Himalayan Nature Park. On the way back, visit the 108-ft statue at Jakhoo Hanuman Temple.

Day03

Viceregal Lodge, Sankat Mochan & Return Drop

Morning visit to the grand British-era Viceregal Lodge and peaceful Sankat Mochan Temple. Afternoon drive back down the Himalayan Expressway with a safe, punctual drop at Zirakpur or Chandigarh.
