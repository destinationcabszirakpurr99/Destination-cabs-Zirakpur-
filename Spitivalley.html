Curated Travel Plan

5-Day Spiti Valley Circuit Itinerary

The ultimate high-altitude mountain expedition starting from Chandigarh / Zirakpur.

Day01

Zirakpur to Reckong Peo / Kalpa

Drive along Sutlej river entering Kinnaur gateway, apple orchards, and majestic Kinner Kailash peak views.

Day02

Kalpa to Nako & Tabo

Visit Nako Lake, cross Khab confluence of Sutlej & Spiti rivers, and explore ancient Tabo Monastery.

Day03

Tabo to Kaza & Key Monastery

Explore Dhankhar Fort, Pin Valley (Mudh), and iconic Key Monastery before reaching Kaza.

Day04

Komic, Hikkim & Langza Excursion

Visit world's highest villages: Komic, Hikkim post office, and Langza fossil village.

Day05

Kaza to Manali / Chandigarh Return

Cross Kunzum Pass (15,060 ft) and Rohtang/Atal Tunnel to return safely back to Chandigarh.
